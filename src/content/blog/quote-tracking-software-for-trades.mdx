Most trades businesses put serious effort into quoting. They visit the site, inspect the problem, price materials, estimate labour, and send a professional PDF.

Then the quote disappears.

The customer might be interested. They might have questions. They might be comparing options. But if nobody follows up, the business has no idea what happened.

Quote tracking software fixes that gap.

## Why quotes go cold

Quotes usually go cold for simple reasons:

- The customer is busy and forgets to reply.
- The quote needs explanation.
- A competitor follows up first.
- The customer is unsure about timing or deposit.
- The business owner forgets to chase the quote.

None of these are unusual. The problem is that most small teams do not have a consistent follow-up process.

## What quote tracking should do

Good quote tracking software should help a trade business understand where every estimate sits.

At minimum, it should show:

- Which quotes are drafted
- Which quotes have been sent
- Which quotes have been viewed
- Which quotes need follow-up
- Which quotes were accepted or rejected

The real value comes when the system recommends or drafts the next action.

## Follow up without sounding pushy

The best quote follow-up is helpful, not aggressive.

For example:

> Hi Sarah, just checking whether you had any questions about the bathroom renovation quote. Happy to walk through the options if that helps.

That message is simple, human, and easy to answer.

An AI follow-up agent can draft messages like this based on the quote type, customer history, and time since the quote was sent.

## Automate the timing, not the relationship

Automation should protect consistency while keeping the relationship personal.

A strong workflow might look like this:

1. Send the quote.
2. Track when the customer opens it.
3. Notify the team if the quote is viewed more than once.
4. Draft a follow-up after 24 or 48 hours.
5. Ask for approval before sending.
6. Move accepted quotes into the booking pipeline.

This keeps the business organized without making the customer feel like they are talking to a robot.

## Why this matters for local businesses

For plumbers, electricians, builders, cleaners, HVAC teams, landscapers, and other service businesses, quote follow-up is one of the easiest ways to increase revenue without buying more leads.

You already paid for the enquiry. You already did the quoting work. The next step is making sure the customer does not fall through the cracks.

## The takeaway

Quote tracking software helps trades businesses turn estimates into a real sales pipeline. When every quote has a status and every customer gets a timely follow-up, fewer jobs disappear after the PDF is sent.
